Help & FAQ

Getting started: Register on the Person page, then find visitors on the Search page.
📝 How do I register as a visitor?
Go to the Person page and fill in the registration form. First name and last name are required and must contain letters only (no numbers). Choose your region and click Register.
🔍 How does the search work?
The search on the Search page matches your keyword across all fields – name, gender, telephone, region, hobbies, and description. Results are highlighted and update in real time as you type.

💾 Where is my data stored?
All visitor records are saved in your browser's localStorage. Data persists across page reloads but is local to your browser and device. Clearing browser data will remove all records.
