Testing Guidelines

  • 1
    Role-based Menu Visibility: Use the "Switch Role" dropdown in the top right to switch between Admin, Teacher, and Student. The sidebar options will filter instantly!
  • 2
    Generic Fragments & URL placeholders: Click any of the sidebar menus. You'll see they are fully operational and load custom titles dynamically!
  • 3
    System Menu Editing: Click the "Edit System Menus" button at the bottom of the sidebar to manage the active links, parent relations, display ordering, and icons.
