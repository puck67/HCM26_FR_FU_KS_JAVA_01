Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a new visitor?

Navigate to the Register Visitor page. Complete the mandatory fields (First name, Last name, and region selection), select any hobbies you have, write a brief description, and click the Register button. A success popup will confirm details upon completion.

How does the search function work?

Type your keyword into the search input on the Search Visitors page and click **Search...**. This advanced search will match your keyword case-insensitively across all fields in the table, not just the first name.

How do I highlight table rows?

Simply click on any row in the table to toggle its light-green background highlight. You can click multiple rows to highlight them, or click them again to remove the highlight.
