Frequently Asked Questions
How do I register a person?

Go to the Person tab, fill out all mandatory fields (marked with an asterisk *), and click the Register button.

How do I use the search function?

Go to the Search tab, enter a keyword in the text field, and click Search. The system will look for matches across all columns (First name, Last name, Phone, Email, Location).
