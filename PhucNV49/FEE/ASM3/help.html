Help Guide

To complete the registration process, the following parameters are enforced:

Field Mandatory Max Length Constraints
First name Yes 20 characters Letters only (no digits/numbers allowed).
Last name Yes 20 characters Letters only (no digits/numbers allowed).
Telephone No 11 characters Digits only (no letters allowed).
Email No 50 characters Must follow standard email formats.
You are in Yes N/A Must select one region (Europe, Africa, Australia, Asia, America).
Description No 200 characters Max length 200 characters.

The **Search Visitor** tool lets you filter the database of registered people in real-time:

  1. Enter any keyword (e.g. name, telephone number, region, hobby, or description term) into the search text field.
  2. Click the **Search...** button.
  3. The system dynamically parses **every cell of every row** in the table, hiding records that do not contain a match and highlighting matches.

Row hovering highlights the details under your cursor with a soft green indicator.
